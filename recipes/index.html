Microwave reheating

For every method: use a microwave-safe bowl, cover it loosely (never sealed tight), and open the cover away from your face because the steam is hot. Leftovers are safe at 165°F.

🍗 Meal-prepped chicken · ~3–4 min

The water, the cover, and the reduced power keep the chicken from drying out. Check with a thermometer instead of going by time alone.

🍚 Cooked rice or rice pilaf · ~2–3 min

Use refrigerated rice within 3–4 days. Reheating doesn't reset that clock. Throw rice away if it sat at room temperature for more than 2 hours after cooking.

🥦 Fresh vegetable medley (refrigerated bag) · ~3–4 min

No butter or oil needed. The bag's own instructions say 3–3½ minutes; a bowl can take a little longer.

❄️ Frozen vegetable medley (½ bag, ~2 cups) · ~4–5 min

Shortcut when making pasta: drop the frozen vegetables into the pasta pot for the last 3–4 minutes of boiling and drain everything together.
